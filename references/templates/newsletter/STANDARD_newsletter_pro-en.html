Bezos' Project Prometheus Nears $10B Close at $38B Valuation

Project Prometheus, co-founded by Jeff Bezos and Vikram Bajaj, is finalizing a $10B round at a $38B valuation with BlackRock and JPMorgan as anchor LPs, only six months after its November 2025 $6.2B seed. The lab's mission — "models that understand the physical world" — integrates world models, foundation robotics models, and industrial agents into a single stack. Cumulative capital deployment now exceeds $16B. This round cements a Q2 2026 structure in which physical-AI capital concentrates into three poles (Prometheus, Figure AI $39B, Waymo), and marks the reallocation inflection from LLM-centric to physical-AI-centric capital within the 80% AI share of Q1's $330.9B global VC total. The Bezos–Bajaj co-CEO structure elevates Prometheus to a second concentration pole approaching Figure AI's tier. Capital now flows across L6 (robotics, autonomous vehicles, industrial agents) feeding world-model training data that reshapes foundation-model competition upstream at L2.

L7 + L5

Cursor $2B Round at $50B Valuation Oversubscribed

Anysphere, Cursor's parent, is negotiating a $2B+ round at $50B pre-money, co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and Thrive Capital, with NVIDIA as a strategic co-investor and Battery Ventures as a new entrant. From $100M ARR (Jan 2025) → $1B (Nov 2025) → $2B (Feb 2026), the 13-month trajectory to $2B ARR is the fastest in B2B SaaS history — surpassing Slack, Zoom, and Snowflake. The round doubles the valuation in five months and is already oversubscribed. This cements winner-take-all in the AI coding-agent layer and intensifies pricing and feature re-entry pressure on GitHub Copilot, Replit, and Codeium. NVIDIA's strategic participation implies committed GPU supply, while a16z and Thrive's co-lead repositions Cursor as an independent stack directly countering Microsoft's GitHub Copilot lock-in. Upstream API consumption at Anthropic and OpenAI accelerates as Cursor's enterprise tier scales, reshaping model-vendor revenue structures via the L7→L2 loop.

Impact 5 · Power +2 (Cursor/Anysphere/a16z) / -1 (Copilot, Replit, Codeium) · Short 0–3mo · L7→L2 active
 

L8 + L7

MATCH Act Revision + GAIN AI Act in NDAA

Senators Ricketts and Kim introduced the MATCH Act on April 8, with a revised draft circulated April 16 that softened the country-wide ban on cryogenic etch and the automatic denial presumption on servicing licenses, while retaining the core prohibition on DUV immersion lithography and cryo etch tools (advanced and legacy nodes) to countries of concern. The GAIN AI Act, folded into the Senate version of the 2026 NDAA, codifies US buyers' right of first refusal — requiring a 15-business-day public notice and certification that no comparable domestic orders remain unfilled before any export license issues. Superimposed on the Jan 15 BIS final rule (H200/MI325X case-by-case + 25% tariff), the congressional layer dismantles pure executive discretion and creates a legal foundation resistant to reversal. Export-license uncertainty now discounts NVIDIA and AMD China revenue with widened Q2 earnings-guidance volatility (L8→L7), while China's domestic chip-substitution demand drives SMIC and Huawei valuation premiums (L8→L1).
