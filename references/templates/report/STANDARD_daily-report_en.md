- **Summary**: Senators Pete Ricketts and Andy Kim introduced the MATCH Act on April 8, with a revised draft circulated April 16 that softened the country-wide ban on cryogenic etch and the automatic denial presumption on servicing licenses, while retaining the core prohibition on DUV immersion lithography and cryo etch tools (advanced and legacy nodes) to countries of concern. The GAIN AI Act, folded into the Senate version of the 2026 NDAA, codifies US buyers' right of first refusal — requiring a 15-business-day public notice and certification that no comparable domestic orders remain unfilled before any export license issues.
